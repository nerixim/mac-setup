# Spark

Look at the current plan (or conversation context if no explicit plan exists) and propose the single smartest, most radically innovative, accretive, useful, and compelling addition you could make to it at this point.

## Constraints

- Exactly one idea. Not a list.
- It must be concrete and actionable — not a vague "consider X".
- Explain *why* this is the highest-leverage addition right now, given the current state of the plan.
- Be bold. Default to surprising and non-obvious over safe and incremental.
- If $ARGUMENTS specifies a focus area or constraint, incorporate it.

## Format

**The spark**: one sentence stating the addition.

**Why this, why now**: 2-3 sentences on why it's the highest-leverage move at this point in the plan.

**What it looks like**: brief sketch of how it would be implemented or integrated.
